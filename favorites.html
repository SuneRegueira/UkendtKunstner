My favorite tracks

Overleve

Overleve is the song i remember first listening to from Ukendt Kunstner. The song has a uplifting but melancholic energy that makes you feel good and gives you hope for the future.

Langt Væk

Langt Væk is the outro on Neonlys, the album that talks about striving for fame. There is no lyrics for the first few minutes. But when they come, they repetitively say in danish: "forget your name, forget where you come from". Sarcastically, claiming that some people forget their roots when they have seen the neon lights

Lige Nu

Lige Nu is the rap heavy anthem from Den Anden Side. The song features a great verse from Danish rapper Sivas. This song is maybe not as introspective as the others but more about being cool and feeling good. The repetitive beat tempts to be almost to much at times, but it is so well done that you can listen to it on repeat anyways.
