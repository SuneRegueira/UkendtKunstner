Forbandede Ungdom

Forbandede Ungdom or in english Cursed Youth. The album shares name with the danish version of the controversial book The Catcher in the Rye which features an anti-establishment teenage protagonist. The same raw and youthful feeling is present throughout the album while the title also relates to the hard time both artits had growing up. The album was released the year after their debut albumGennem Byen is the thoughtful and introspective song that gets you in your feels, especially when you are in fact driving through the city, just like in the song but also shown aesthetically in the music video.

Neonlys

Neonlys is the first officially released album. The album reflects about fame and partying and the feeling the day after. With a heavy almost disco-production at times classics like Neonlys are unavoidable on the dancefloor even a decade after. The title track of Neonlys is their most ever listened song. The song talks about the hunger for fame and backside here of.

Dansktop

Dansktop is the return album of the duo released in 2023. The instrumentals have a different vibe and the lyrics talking about being too old to party and in love shows how they have matured over the years especially taking the break for 7 years. The lead single of their return album in 2023, they decided to make a love song and turn it into a straight banger. Enjoy a short bite of Uden Dig here!
